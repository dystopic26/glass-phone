                Good to know

                • Trial accounts can only text and call numbers you've verified in Twilio, and calls start with a short Twilio trial message.
                • Texting US numbers from a paid account needs your number registered (toll-free verification or A2P 10DLC), or carriers may block messages.
                • Audio calls to cell phones need the person's OK to receive recorded messages.
                • Uploaded clips are stored in your Twilio account at hard-to-guess web links, so only someone with the exact link can play them.
                • Shortcuts: ⌘1–5 switch tabs, ⌘N new message, ⌘F search. On a call, type digits to use the keypad.
